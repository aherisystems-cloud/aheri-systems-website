The 4-Phase Framework

From audit to optimization—here's exactly how we build your Revenue Command System

1

Revenue Leakage Audit

Week 0 - Free

What Happens:

"We map your current lead-to-customer flow and identify exactly where revenue is leaking."

For Real Estate:
  • How many inquiries do you get per month?
  • What's your current response time?
  • How many leads get followed up? How many don't?
  • What's your inquiry-to-viewing conversion rate?
  • What's your viewing-to-offer rate?
  • Where are deals stalling?
For Private Clinics:
  • How many patient inquiries per month?
  • What's your appointment booking rate?
  • What's your no-show rate?
  • How many patients return for follow-ups?
  • Where are patients falling off?

Deliverable:

Free Revenue Leakage Report showing exactly where you're losing money and how much it's costing you.

Book Your Free Audit
2

System Design

Week 1-2

What Happens:

"We design the exact workflows, automation, and enforcement mechanisms your business needs—customized for your team size, structure, and goals."

What We Map Out:

  • Lead/patient capture points (all channels)
  • Response automation (immediate AI response + agent/staff handoff)
  • Follow-up sequences (automated drip campaigns)
  • Pipeline/appointment management (visual tracking)
  • Team assignments and accountability
  • Reporting and dashboards

Deliverable:

Complete System Blueprint—you see exactly what's being built before we build it.

Timeline:

2 weeks from audit to blueprint approval

3

Build & Activation

Week 3-6

What Happens:

"We build your Revenue Command System, integrate it with your existing tools (WhatsApp, social media, etc.), test it thoroughly, and train your entire team."

What Gets Built:

For Real Estate:
  • Centralized lead capture (website forms, WhatsApp, Facebook, calls)
  • AI lead response system (instant 24/7 response)
  • CRM with visual deal pipeline
  • Automated follow-up sequences
  • Agent dashboards
  • Commission tracking
  • Weekly reporting
For Private Clinics:
  • Online booking system (patients book 24/7)
  • Patient CRM with appointment tracking
  • Automated appointment reminders (SMS/WhatsApp)
  • Post-visit follow-up sequences
  • Patient health scoring
  • Staff dashboards
  • Weekly patient flow reports

Training:

"Your entire team gets hands-on training. We don't leave until everyone knows how to use the system."

Timeline:

3-4 weeks from blueprint approval to full activation

4

Ongoing Optimization

Week 7+

What Happens:

"We don't build and disappear. We monitor your system continuously, send weekly reports, and optimize monthly based on real data."

What You Get:

  • Weekly Revenue Reports (every Monday morning)
    Real Estate: Leads captured, response times, pipeline movement, deals closing
    Clinics: Inquiries, bookings, attendance rate, follow-up completion
  • Monthly Strategy Sessions (Tier 2 & 3)
    Review performance metrics, identify bottlenecks, optimize workflows, plan improvements
  • Quarterly Business Reviews (Tier 3)
    Deep dive into ROI, growth planning, system enhancements

Timeline:

Ongoing—this is a partnership, not a one-time project
