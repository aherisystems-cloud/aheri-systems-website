The 4-Phase Framework

From audit to optimization—here's exactly how we build your Revenue Command System

1

Revenue Leakage Audit

Week 0 - Free

What Happens:

We map your current lead-to-customer flow and identify exactly where revenue is leaking.

Questions We Ask (Any Industry):

  • How many inquiries do you get per month?
  • What's your current response time?
  • How many leads get followed up? How many don't?
  • What's your inquiry-to-sale conversion rate?
  • Where are deals/opportunities stalling?
  • How many customers return for repeat business?
  • What percentage of team time is admin vs revenue-generating?

Deliverable:

Free Revenue Leakage Report showing exactly where you're losing money and how much it's costing you.

2

System Design

Week 1-2

What Happens:

We design the exact workflows, automation, and enforcement mechanisms your business needs—AND we recommend the best platform for your situation.

Platform Selection Discussion:

  • Already using a CRM? We'll assess if we should optimize it or migrate
  • Budget-conscious? We'll recommend cost-effective options (Zoho, GoHighLevel)
  • Need enterprise features? We'll consider HubSpot or Salesforce
  • Want our platform? We'll deploy that
  • Starting fresh? We'll recommend based on your industry and team size

What We Map Out:

  • Lead/customer capture points (all channels)
  • Response automation (AI + team handoff)
  • Follow-up sequences
  • Pipeline/tracking management
  • Team assignments & accountability
  • Reporting & dashboards

Deliverable:

Complete System Blueprint + Platform Recommendation—you see exactly what's being built before we build it.

3

Build & Activation

Week 3-6

What Happens:

We build your Revenue Command System on the platform that best fits your needs, integrate with existing tools, test thoroughly, and train your team.

Built On World-Class Platforms:

  • Our proprietary platform
  • Zoho (budget-friendly, powerful for SMEs)
  • GoHighLevel (all-in-one marketing + CRM)
  • HubSpot (enterprise-grade)
  • ActiveCampaign (marketing automation)
  • Odoo (ERP + CRM)

What Gets Built (Customized to Your Industry):

  • Centralized lead/customer capture
  • AI response system (instant 24/7)
  • CRM with visual tracking
  • Automated follow-up sequences
  • Team dashboards & performance tracking
  • Weekly reporting

Training:

Your entire team gets hands-on training. We don't leave until everyone knows how to use the system.

Timeline:

3-4 weeks from blueprint approval to full activation

4

Ongoing Optimization

Week 7+

What Happens:

We don't build and disappear. We monitor your system continuously, send weekly reports, and optimize monthly based on real data.

What You Get:

  • Weekly Revenue Reports (every Monday morning): Leads captured, response times, pipeline movement, conversion metrics
  • Monthly Strategy Sessions: Review performance metrics, identify bottlenecks, optimize workflows, plan improvements
  • Continuous Improvement: We add 2-3 new automations monthly to keep smoothing operations

Timeline:

Ongoing—this is a partnership, not a one-time project
