Revenue Doesn't Leak Because of Bad Marketing

It leaks in three places

Leak #1: Marketing/Sales

Leads Scatter & Disappear

  • Inquiries across WhatsApp, calls, social media, website
  • Response time: 2-6 hours (competitor responds in 5 minutes)
  • 30-40% of leads never get followed up
  • After hours? Nothing happens
Leak #2: Conversion

Follow-Up is Forgotten

  • Team forgets to follow up (no reminders, no automation)
  • Hot prospects go cold
  • Zero visibility: "How many quotes sent? How many followed up?"
  • Deals stall and die in the "pipeline"
Leak #3: Operations

Customers Buy Once, Disappear

  • No retention system
  • No re-engagement campaigns
  • Complaints get lost (email, WhatsApp chaos)
  • Every customer is a NEW customer (expensive)

Every lost inquiry is revenue walking away.
Every missed follow-up is money you can't recover.
