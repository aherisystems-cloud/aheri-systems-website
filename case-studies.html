Healthcare

VivaLife Pediatric Clinic

How we transformed a pediatric clinic's patient experience through complete CRM automation

Industry Healthcare
Service CRM & Patient Journey
Timeline 6 weeks

The Challenge

Problem: VivaLife was operating with completely manual patient registration processes. Every new patient required staff to manually input information, create files, schedule appointments, and send reminders. This led to:

  • Long wait times for patients during check-in
  • Frequent data entry errors and inconsistencies
  • Missed appointments due to manual reminder systems
  • Staff spending 60% of their time on administrative tasks
  • No clear view of patient history or analytics

The Solution

We implemented a complete healthcare CRM system with automated patient journey workflows:

  • Digital Patient Registration: Online forms that auto-populate the CRM
  • Automated Appointment Booking: Self-service scheduling integrated with clinic calendar
  • Smart Reminders: Automated SMS and email reminders 48 hours and 2 hours before appointments
  • Patient Portal: Secure access to medical records and test results
  • Follow-up Automation: Post-visit surveys and check-in sequences
  • Real-time Analytics: Dashboard showing patient volume, demographics, and appointment trends

Tools & Technologies

CRM Platform Automated Workflows SMS Integration Email Automation Analytics Dashboard Patient Portal

The Results

50% Time Saved on Admin Tasks
95% Appointment Attendance Rate
100% Data Accuracy
24/7 Self-Service Booking

"The automation systems Rita implemented have transformed how we operate. We've cut admin time in half and can now focus on patient care. The real-time analytics give us insights we never had before."

— VivaLife Pediatric Clinic Team

Industry HealthTech
Service CRM Implementation
Timeline 8 weeks

The Challenge

Problem: DiaspoCare needed to coordinate care across multiple healthcare providers while maintaining a seamless patient experience. They faced:

  • Manual coordination between specialists and primary care

  • Difficulty tracking referrals and follow-ups

The Solution

We implemented GoHighLevel as the central CRM automation hub:

  • Appointment Coordination: Centralized scheduling across all providers
  • Patient Journey Tracking: Complete visibility from first contact to ongoing care
  • Provider Dashboard: Real-time view of their patient queue and tasks
